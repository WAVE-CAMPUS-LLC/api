Good to have you!

WAVE. ("WAVE.", "we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy and security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you use the WAVE. mobile application and any related services (collectively, the "Services"). By accessing or using the Services, you agree to be bound by this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree to this Privacy Policy, you may not access or use the Services.

Information We Collect
  • Information You Provide
    1. Account Information: When you register for an account, we may collect information such as your name, email address, date of birth, and password.
    2. Ticket Purchases: When you purchase tickets through the Services, we may collect information such as your payment information, billing address, and ticket preferences.
    3. Communications: We may collect information when you communicate with us or event organizers through the Services, such as your messages, feedback, or customer support inquiries.
  • Information We Collect Automatically
    1. Usage Data: We may collect information about how you access and use the Services, including your interactions with event listings, ticket purchases, and promotions.
    2. Device Information: We may collect information about your device, such as your device type, operating system, browser type, IP address, and mobile network information.
    3. Location Information: With your consent, we may collect information about your location using GPS, Wi-Fi, or other technologies to provide location-based services, such as event recommendations and venue directions.
Use of Information

We may use the information we collect for various purposes, including to:

  • Provide and maintain the Services, including processing ticket purchases and delivering tickets to you;
  • Customize and personalize your experience, including recommending events and promotions that may be of interest to you;
  • Communicate with you about events, promotions, and updates related to the Services;
  • Analyze usage trends and improve the Services, including optimizing event listings and ticketing processes;
  • Protect the security and integrity of the Services, including detecting and preventing fraud or unauthorized access.

Disclosure of Information

We may disclose your information to third parties for various purposes, including to:

  • Event Organizers: We may share your information with event organizers to facilitate ticket purchases, event check-in, and communication related to events you attend.
  • Service Providers: We may share your information with third-party service providers who help us operate, maintain, and improve the Services, such as payment processors and marketing partners.
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose your information in response to a subpoena, court order, or other legal request, or to comply with applicable laws or regulations.

Data Retention

We will retain your information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the security and integrity of your information, but no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee the absolute security of your information.

Children's Privacy

You may update or delete your account information at any time by accessing your account settings. You may also opt out of receiving promotional communications from us by following the instructions provided in such communications.

Changes to this Privacy Policy
