Get ready for an unforgettable night of music and fun at our annual Campus Concert Extravaganza! Join us on April 30th, 2024 at 7:00 PM at the Gentry Center on Tennessee State University campus for an electrifying evening filled with live performances, vibrant energy, and unforgettable memories.


Headlining Acts

We are thrilled to present an incredible lineup of artists who will be rocking the stage. Headlining the concert are: The Electric Waves: Known for their chart-topping hits and dynamic stage presence, they are set to deliver a performance that will leave you wanting more.
Luna and the Comets: With their unique sound and captivating lyrics, this band is sure to get you dancing and singing along.
Echo Horizon: Kicking off the night, this talented group will set the tone with their energetic and heartfelt performance.




Event Highlights
Food and Drinks: Enjoy a variety of delicious food trucks and beverage stations available throughout the venue. From classic festival fare to gourmet treats, there's something for everyone. Interactive Zones: Explore our interactive zones featuring fun activities, photo booths, and merchandise stands where you can grab your favorite band’s memorabilia. VIP Experience: Upgrade to a VIP ticket for exclusive access to front-row seating, a private lounge, meet-and-greet opportunities with the artists, and more.


Additional Information
Tickets: Purchase your tickets in advance at [Ticket Link] to secure your spot. Student discounts are available with a valid ID. Safety Measures: Your safety is our priority. The event will adhere to all current health guidelines, including sanitation stations and socially distanced seating arrangements.


Join Us

Follow us on social media [Social Media Handles] for updates, giveaways, and more exciting announcements leading up to the event. Directions & Parking

The Gentry Center is conveniently located on Tennessee State University's campus at 3500 John A Merritt Blvd, Nashville, TN 37209. Free parking is available in designated lots adjacent to the venue. Follow the signs on campus for concert parking. We encourage carpooling or using rideshare services to help ease traffic congestion.




Accessibility

We are committed to making our event accessible to everyone. The Gentry Center is wheelchair accessible, and designated seating areas will be available for individuals with disabilities. If you have specific accessibility needs or require assistance, please contact our event staff at [Contact Information] prior to the event.




What to Bring

Tickets: Ensure you have your printed ticket or e-ticket ready for scanning at the entrance. ID: Valid identification is required for student ticket holders and for purchasing alcoholic beverages. Comfortable Attire: Wear comfortable clothing and shoes, as you’ll likely be standing, dancing, and moving around. Blankets & Chairs: You can bring blankets or portable chairs for seating in designated areas. Prohibited Items For safety and enjoyment of all attendees, the following items are prohibited at the event: Outside food and beverages Large bags or backpacks Weapons of any kind Illegal substances Professional cameras or recording equipment Food & Beverages A variety of food trucks and beverage stations will be available throughout the venue, offering a range of options from classic festival fare to gourmet treats. Vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free options will also be available.




Weather Policy

The concert will take place rain or shine. In the event of severe weather, updates will be provided on our social media channels and event website. Please dress appropriately for the weather conditions, and consider bringing rain gear if necessary.




Safety Measures

Your safety is our priority. The event will adhere to all current health guidelines, including: Sanitation Stations: Hand sanitizing stations will be available throughout the venue. Social Distancing: Please follow marked guidelines and staff instructions for socially distanced seating and lines. Face Coverings: Masks are recommended, especially in crowded areas.




Contact Information

If you have any questions or need further information, please contact us at [Contact Information] or visit our website at [Event Website]. Our team is here to ensure you have a fantastic experience at the Campus Concert Extravaganza.




We look forward to seeing you there for an unforgettable night!
