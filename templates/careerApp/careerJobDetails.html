At WAVE., we're on a mission to revolutionize the way college students connect and engage with entertainment and events on a global scale. Our platform is designed to empower students to discover and participate in a vibrant array of experiences, bringing them closer to their peers and the excitement happening both locally and worldwide. We're a dynamic team of college students ourselves, driven by our shared passion for creating unforgettable moments and fostering a sense of community among our users.

As a Social Media Manager at WAVE., you'll play a pivotal role in amplifying our brand and expanding our reach across various social media platforms. You'll be at the forefront of shaping our online presence, crafting compelling content, and engaging with our audience in meaningful ways. Whether it's sharing the latest event highlights, sparking conversations around trending topics, or fostering connections between students from different corners of the globe, you'll be instrumental in bringing the WAVE. experience to life.

If you're passionate about harnessing the power of social media to connect students and create unforgettable experiences, we want to hear from you. Join us at WAVE. and be part of a team that's making waves in the world of college entertainment and events.

What's expected of you
  • Develop and execute a comprehensive social media content strategy aligned with our brand identity and target audience. Create engaging and relevant content, including graphics, videos, and written posts, to promote events, drive user engagement, and foster community interaction.
  • Manage our presence across various social media platforms, including but not limited to Instagram, Twitter, Facebook, and TikTok. Regularly update and optimize profiles, monitor trends, and stay abreast of platform updates and algorithm changes to maximize reach and engagement.
  • Proactively engage with our social media audience by responding to comments, messages, and mentions in a timely and personable manner. Foster a sense of community by facilitating conversations, encouraging user-generated content, and showcasing user experiences.
  • Identify and cultivate relationships with relevant influencers, student organizations, and potential brand partners to amplify our reach and engagement. Collaborate on co-promotional initiatives, sponsored content, and influencer partnerships to broaden our presence within the college community.
What you need to have
  • Develop and execute a social media strategy aligned with WAVE's goals, regularly evaluating performance metrics to refine tactics and ensure alignment with objectives.
  • Generate engaging multimedia content tailored to diverse platforms and audience interests, while also curating user-generated content and leveraging trending topics to maximize reach and resonance.
  • Serve as the primary liaison between WAVE and its social media audience, fostering positive interactions, addressing inquiries promptly, and proactively engaging with users to cultivate a vibrant and inclusive community.

At WAVE., we're revolutionizing event ticketing with a key focus on college campuses. Our mission is to bring people together for unforgettable experiences, starting with the vibrant communities on college campuses worldwide. We're dedicated to fostering genuine human connections through our app. We believe that college campuses are hubs of creativity, diversity, and excitement, and we're committed to making event-going more accessible and enjoyable for students everywhere. With a rapidly growing user base and ambitious goals, WAVE. is poised to become a leader in the event ticketing industry, starting with our focus on college campuses. We're looking for passionate individuals who share our vision of bringing people together through live entertainment. If you're excited about the prospect of positively impacting millions of students worldwide, join us in building the future of event ticketing at WAVE.
